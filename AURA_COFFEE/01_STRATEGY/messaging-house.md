## THE ROOF — THE PROPOSITION

# REGENERATIVE COFFEE

**Definition (the line we always return to):**
Coffee that leaves the land better than it found it.

**Signature:**
One remarkable circle.

Nothing else competes with this. Biodynamic farming, the herd, the lab, the ferments, the record — none of them is a proposition. Each is a proof point sitting under a pillar. If a piece of copy makes the cows the story, or the blockchain the story, or the science the story, it is wrong and must be rewritten.

## THE THREE PILLARS

The pillars come straight from the founder's card. They are the right three, in the right order, and they should not be renamed.

### 01 — BIODYNAMIC
*How it is grown.*

**Pillar line:** Grown in a closed loop.

**Core thought:** The estate feeds itself. The herd feeds the soil, the soil feeds the trees, nothing comes in and nothing leaves.

**Proof points**
- 52 Malnad Gidda — an indigenous Karnataka breed adapted to this altitude and these grasses. Their dung is the raw material for every preparation on the estate.
- ~154,000 litres of Jeevamrit brewed a year, stirred by hand.
- ~2,420 kg of Cow Pat Pit a year across 14 numbered pits, matured 90 days.
- BD 500–508 alongside the Vedic set — Jeevamrit, Panchgavya, Beejamrit, CPP, Kunapjal.
- A four-story canopy: silver oak and fig above, areca and hardwood, coffee, then pepper and cover crop at ground level. 35,000 trees.
- Shade managed by measurement — 50 lux readings per acre before a single branch comes down.
- Nothing is bought in. Nothing is trucked away.

**What this pillar must never say:** that Aura is certified organic or certified biodynamic. It is not. See [claims-and-proof.md](claims-and-proof.md).

---

### 02 — TRANSPARENT
*How it is proved.*

**Pillar line:** Written down as it happens.

**Core thought:** Every claim on this page has a record behind it, taken at the moment it was true, signed by the person who did the work.

**Proof points**
- Nine data streams off the estate, each on its own clock — pH every 15 minutes through a ferment, soil at 5 cm and 20 cm each quarter, canopy NDVI quarterly.
- A single record reads: *BD 501 applied to Block 07 at 06:14, waning moon, humidity 78%, by Raju, from dung batch G-03.*
- Every animal carries a passport — health, milk, urine daily; dung logged per herd each morning.
- Every preparation batch is tested before and after — pH, EC, microbial colony counts, *Trichoderma*, *Pseudomonas*. A failing batch is corrected or held, never sprayed.
- The block that received it is retested 90 days on.
- Every lot's file: harvest weight, immature removed, floats removed, Brix, water TDS, ferment hours, drying days.
- Plain JSON and CSV — files that will still open in a decade.
- **Aura also publishes what it does not yet know.** The Solera carry-forward may or may not hold a house culture across seasons. Aura says so, in writing, on its own site. That is the transparency pillar working.

**What this pillar must never say:** that on-chain traceability is live, until a public explorer link exists. Until then it is `[FUTURE]`.

---

### 03 — FLAVOURFUL
*Why any of it matters.*

**Pillar line:** Six lots. One harvest. No two cups the same.

**Core thought:** Flavour is accumulated consequence. The cup is the readout of a year of decisions.

**Proof points**
- Arabica Sln.9 and Sln.795 at 3,600 ft, under 60–80% canopy.
- Blocks held at 65–75% canopy cover cup measurably better.
- Six micro-lots from one harvest: Anaerobic Natural, Dry Osmosis, Red Honey ("Liquid Gold"), Banana Wash, Solera Maceration, Solera Wash.
- Minimum 95% ripeness at pick. All floats removed. Ferment ends at pH 4.2.
- Brix from 18% to 23% across the lots — Solera Wash the highest.
- Named notes from the estate's own cupping: blueberry, fig and raisin on Dry Osmosis; honey, caramel and nut on Red Honey.
- Banana Wash — 24 hours anaerobic, then layered under banana leaves for 48 more. The most distinctly Indian lot on the estate.

**What this pillar must never say:** a cupping score Aura has not received. 80+ SCA is the target, not a result. `[NEEDS PROOF]`

## AUDIENCE CUTS

Same proposition, different entry point. Never a different story.

### The roaster / green buyer
**Entry:** the lot file.
**What they need:** separation, consistency, documentation, allocation terms, forward contracts.
**Lead line:** *Six lots. One harvest. Every one of them documented to the hour.*
**Proof to lead with:** ferment logs, Brix, screen grading and defect analysis per SCA protocol, GrainPro packing, the QR record.
**What not to do:** open with the cows. Open with the coffee, then earn the cows.

### The coffee drinker
**Entry:** the smile.
**What they need:** a reason to care that does not feel like homework.
**Lead line:** *Regenerative coffee. Is that a thing?*
**Proof to lead with:** the circle, the cow, the six ferments, the taste.
**What not to do:** lead with soil carbon.

### The chef / hotel / sanctuary
**Entry:** provenance as a story they can retell at the table.
**Lead line:** *One estate. One harvest. Six ways.*
**Proof to lead with:** the named lots, "Liquid Gold", the estate visit.

### The press / partner / investor
**Entry:** the model, not the romance.
**Lead line:** *The land gets the better deal.*
**Proof to lead with:** the closed loop, the Ecological Health Index, the record architecture, the honesty about what is unproven.

## TONE OF VOICE — RULES OF THUMB

**Do**
- British English.
- One strong sentence over one paragraph.
- Observation over explanation.
- Concrete nouns and real numbers: *fourteen pits, 06:14, pH 4.2, banana leaves.*
- Say what a thing **is**. Never "it is not X, it is Y."
- Admit the limits. It is the most persuasive move available.
- Occasional wit, always dry, never cute.

**Don't**
- *Journey, passion, commitment, sustainable future, premium, eco-friendly, conscious, revolutionary, innovative solution.*
- "We believe…" more than once in an entire body of work.
- Adjective stacking. One adjective, or none.
- Exclamation marks.
- Present ambition in the present tense.

**The test.** Read the sentence aloud. If it sounds like it came from a report, cut it. If it sounds like it came from someone standing in the field at six in the morning, keep it.
