## THE ELEVEN THAT MUST BE SHOT

In order. The first four change what the site *is*; the rest make it better.

1. **The cupping table, overhead** — the payoff of the entire argument
2. **The lux meter, number legible** — makes "measure first" visible
3. **Signing from the field** — makes the record human
4. **One lab report, dated** — converts the middle pillar from claim to evidence
5. **The hand-drawn ring and three underlines, vectorised** — the whole visual system
6. **The stir, one unbroken take** — site and film both
7. **The ear tag, macro** — the passport in one frame
8. **Breaking the crust** — the sensory close
9. **December first pick** — hands and red cherry
10. **Banana leaves over cherry** — the most distinctive lot's missing hero
11. **Ferment foam, macro** — already promised in existing copy, never shot

**A before/after pair of one regenerated block** sits outside this list because it may not be possible — but if a dated pair exists in anyone's phone, it is worth more than items 6 through 11 combined.

## SPECIFICATIONS

**Photography.** Available light. Dawn and late afternoon; the canopy makes midday flat and green. Shoot for the site's dark register — expose for highlight, let shadows go. No flash on animals or people at work. Landscape and 4:5 portrait of every hero, since the film cutdowns and the deck need both.

**Video.** 4K, 24 fps, log. Locked-off or slow deliberate movement — no gimbal swoops. Every clip must survive being cut to a six-second silent loop. Record clean audio on everything, even shots that will run muted: the film's sound design depends on it.

**Deliverables per asset.** Original · web-optimised (AVIF/WebP, ≤1600 px long edge) · 4:5 crop · 16:9 crop · alt text written by whoever was there.

**Consent.** Written release from every named individual — Nayana, Jagadeshwari, Raju, Dr Arun, and every worker whose face is identifiable. Non-negotiable before publication.

**Naming.** Follow the existing convention: `aura-{subject}-{detail}.{ext}`, lower case, hyphenated. Store under `/public/coffee/` for this project so it does not collide with the journals.
