## 21

**And here is what we can't prove.**

*We carry a fraction of a fermenting batch forward into fresh cherry, the way a sherry cellar carries its culture. Can a coffee ferment hold a house culture from one season to the next? Nobody has shown it. We're testing it, year by year.*

***We'd rather tell you that than sell you the metaphor.***

**Visual:** No image. Type on a plain ground.
**Purpose:** **The deck's centre of gravity.** Deliver it flat, without apology and without pride. Do not smile. Do not soften it. This slide buys more trust than the fifteen before it.
**Proof:** Aura's own published position. Coffee ferments are short; no strain has been demonstrated to persist between seasons.
