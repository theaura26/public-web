# Keynote — Story Structure
## Aura Regenerative Coffee · The Master Deck

---

## WHAT THIS DECK IS

The source of truth. The microsite is the front door and the film is the invitation; this is the document that holds the whole argument, and every other piece of work is cut from it.

It has to survive two lives:

**Presented live** — 20 to 25 minutes, one voice, a room of roasters, buyers, partners or press. Slides carry almost no text because the speaker is the text.

**Sent as a PDF** — read alone, in silence, on a laptop, by someone deciding whether to reply. It must work with nobody talking over it.

Those two demands usually pull in opposite directions. The resolution is structural: **the slides carry the argument, the notes carry the detail.** A speaker ignores the notes. A reader gets them as the deck's second layer, and a PDF export with notes visible is the leave-behind.

## THE SHAPE — 32 SLIDES, FIVE ACTS

```
   OPEN          01–05    The question, and the turn                5 slides
   ══ BIODYNAMIC ══
   ACT ONE       06–15    How it is grown                          10 slides
   ══ TRANSPARENT ══
   ACT TWO       16–22    How it is proved — including what isn't    7 slides
   ══ FLAVOURFUL ══
   ACT THREE     23–28    Why any of it matters                     6 slides
   CLOSE         29–32    The circle, and the invitation             4 slides
```

The three acts are the three pillars, in the order they appear on the founder's card and in the site navigation. Same architecture, three media. That consistency is not tidiness — it is what makes the campaign feel like one idea rather than three commissions.

## THE RHYTHM

A deck that presents thirty-two slides at one pace is a document, not a talk. This one moves in deliberate gear changes.

| Slide type | Count | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent** — one image, no words | 6 | The speaker stops talking. Three to five seconds. Uncomfortable, on purpose |
| **Statement** — one line, black or white ground | 9 | Read aloud, then a beat |
| **Evidence** — data only, mono type | 7 | The speaker slows down and gets specific |
| **Photographic** — image with a short caption | 7 | The estate does the talking |
| **Turn** — a surprise, a reversal, or an admission | 3 | The three slides people remember |

**The three turns** are slides 05, 21 and 28. Everything else is built to make them land:

- **05** — *Coffee doesn't begin with coffee.* The audience arrives expecting a coffee talk and gets a soil talk.
- **21** — *And here is what we can't prove.* Nobody in the room has seen a producer do this.
- **28** — *The same cherry, six ways.* The moment the argument becomes appetite.

## THE OPENING FIVE

The most important ninety seconds. It must not begin with an agenda slide, a logo, or a photograph of a beautiful farm.

```
01   Aura presents.                              (silent, black)
02   Regenerative Coffee.                        (statement)
03   Is that a thing?                            (handwriting, held)
04   Coffee that leaves the land better          (statement)
     than it found it.
05   Coffee doesn't begin with coffee.           (turn)
```

Slide 03 should sit on screen for an uncomfortable length of time. The speaker says nothing. The room answers the question in its own head — some sceptically — and that private answer is what the next twenty minutes is arguing with.

**Never open with the cows.** They are the best material in the deck and they are worthless on slide 02, because nobody yet knows why an animal is in a coffee presentation.

## HOW EACH ACT IS BUILT

### ACT ONE — BIODYNAMIC (06–15)

**The job:** make a closed loop feel inevitable rather than idealistic.

**The sequence:** land → herd → loop → light. Ground first, because it is the only thing in the deck nobody can argue with. Then the animal, which is the surprise. Then the mechanism, which is the payoff. Then the light, which proves the method is measured rather than believed.

**The act's own turn is slide 09** — *Our best farmers have four legs.* By this point the audience has had three slides of geology and is not expecting a joke.

**Ends on:** the loop diagram, held silent.

### ACT TWO — TRANSPARENT (16–22)

**The job:** convert the room from *this is charming* to *this is real*.

**The sequence:** nine data streams → one signed record, in full → the testing rule → soil re-read at ninety days → **the admission** → the ledger quote.

This act is deliberately the driest in the deck, and that is correct. It should feel like the speaker has stopped performing and started showing their working. The energy drop is the point — it makes act three's return to the senses land as relief.

**Its turn, slide 21, is the deck's centre of gravity.** A producer standing in front of buyers saying *we do not know whether this works, and we are testing it* does more for credibility than every number that precedes it.

### ACT THREE — FLAVOURFUL (23–28)

**The job:** make them want to taste it.

**The sequence:** the discipline (95% ripeness, floats out, pH 4.2) → the six lots → the honest footnote about ceilings → the cup.

Almost no data. Almost no argument. Names, methods and sensory language. The slides get simpler as the act goes on, and slide 28 — *no two cups the same* — is the last thing said before the close.

### CLOSE (29–32)

```
29   The circle, completing               (silent)
30   Yes, it is.                          (handwriting)
31   Aura presents / Regenerative Coffee  (lock-up)
     / One remarkable circle.
32   The Experience — Sep · Dec · Jan     (invitation)
```

**Slide 32 is the only ask in thirty-two slides.** Three months, one email address. Not a pipeline, not a partnership framework, not next steps.

## WHAT THIS DECK REFUSES TO BE

- **No agenda slide.** Nobody has ever been persuaded by a table of contents.
- **No "the problem with the industry" section.** Aura cannot source those comparative claims — see [claims-and-proof.md](../01_STRATEGY/claims-and-proof.md) — and a deck that attacks the category sounds defensive. The conventional model is described once, in a single line, as *what we chose not to do*.
- **No sustainability framework.** No pillars-of-ESG diagram, no UN goals, no pledge.
- **No org chart, no roadmap, no market sizing.** Those belong in an investor deck, which this is not. If one is needed, it is a separate document that borrows slides 06–22.
- **No slide with more than twenty-five words.** If a thought needs more, it is two slides or it is a speaker note.
- **No certification logos.** There are none, and the deck says so plainly on slide 22.

## THE THREE VERSIONS

The deck is built once and cut three ways. Nothing is rewritten.

| Version | Slides | For | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master** | All 32 | The full talk, and the PDF | 20–25 min |
| **Trade** | 01–05, 16–28, 31–32 | Roasters and green buyers who want proof and cup, not romance | 12 min |
| **Short** | 01–05, 09, 14, 21, 28, 30–32 | A twelve-slide version for a meeting, a screen, or a stranger | 5 min |

The short version is the one that will actually get forwarded. Build it deliberately rather than by deleting slides in a hurry.

## SPEAKING NOTES — HOW TO USE THEM

Every slide in [keynote-copy.md](keynote-copy.md) carries a **Purpose** line and a **Proof/source** line.

**Purpose** tells the speaker what the slide is for, so they can improvise around it without losing the thread.

**Proof/source** is the line that gets read aloud if someone challenges the claim in the room — and it is also what makes the PDF stand alone. Where a claim is not yet provable, that line says so. A speaker who has read this column cannot accidentally oversell the estate, which is the single greatest risk in presenting this material.
