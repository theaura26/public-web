# Film Extensions
## Aura Regenerative Coffee

**The rule the brief set, and it is the right one:** do not shorten the master film. Each format gets its own idea, built from the same footage.

A 60-second version of a film whose entire structure is a two-and-a-half-minute reversal is just a broken two-and-a-half-minute film. So the cutdowns don't attempt the reversal. Each one takes a single moment and lets it be the whole thing.

---

## 60 SECONDS — "THE STIR"

**The idea:** one man, one barrel, one minute. Almost the entire runtime is the shot everybody else would cut away from.

**Structure**
```
0:00–0:06   The cup, top-down. Steam.
0:06–0:44   THE STIR. Unbroken, or as close as the footage allows.
            Water turning, wood on steel, breathing.
            No voiceover for 30 seconds.
0:44–0:50   VO: "This is how we make fertility.
                 We don't buy it."
0:50–0:56   The cow, held. She looks back.
0:56–1:00   Lock-up. "One remarkable circle."
```

**Why it works:** it is a dare. Thirty seconds of a man stirring a barrel is either the most boring thing in the feed or the most arresting, and at this length there is no middle. Whoever stays has understood the brand completely.

**Where it runs:** YouTube pre-roll (skippable — the length is the filter), the microsite's Act One, trade presentations, the estate visit.

---

## 30 SECONDS — "SIX WAYS"

**The idea:** drop the farming entirely. This is the appetite cut.

**Structure**
```
0:00–0:04   Hands picking. Two taken, three left.
0:04–0:20   Six lots, six images, ~2.5 sec each.
            Each with its name in mono, lower left:
            ANAEROBIC NATURAL · DRY OSMOSIS · RED HONEY
            BANANA WASH · SOLERA MACERATION · SOLERA WASH
0:20–0:26   VO: "The same cherry, picked the same morning.
                 Six different coffees."
0:26–0:30   The cup. Lock-up.
```

**Why it works:** it is the only cut in the system that leads with flavour, and it is the one a roaster will forward to a colleague. No cows, no soil, no argument — just the six names and the fact that they came off the same tree.

**Where it runs:** trade, roaster outreach, the `/lots` page header, Instagram feed.

---

## 15 SECONDS — "FOUR LEGS"

**The idea:** the joke, delivered straight, with no setup.

**Structure**
```
0:00–0:06   The cow. Held. She looks at the lens
            and keeps looking. No cut. No music.
0:06–0:10   VO: "Our best farmers have four legs."
0:10–0:13   One cut: the coffee cherry on the branch.
0:13–0:15   Lock-up.
```

**Why it works:** six seconds of an animal staring at you is genuinely funny, and the line lands on a viewer who has already started to smile. It is the single most shareable asset in the whole system, and it contains no claim that needs proving.

**Where it runs:** everywhere. This is the top of the funnel.

## 6-SECOND BUMPERS — FOUR OF THEM

Non-skippable, sound-off-safe, each ending on the ring closing.

| # | Picture | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **01** | The vortex, mid-turn | *Regenerative coffee.* / *Is that a thing?* |
| **02** | The cow, one second of eye contact | *Our best farmers have four legs.* |
| **03** | Ferment foam lifting | *A week in the dark.* |
| **04** | The cup, top-down, steam | *One remarkable circle.* |

Each is one image and one line. No voiceover — these have to work muted. The ring closes in the final 20 frames of all four.

## VERTICAL SOCIAL — 9:16

**Not crops.** Three purpose-built vertical edits, each shot with a vertical frame in mind on the day.

### V1 — "The stir" · 45 sec
The vertical frame is a gift here: the barrel fills the bottom two-thirds, the man fills the top. Text appears at 0:03 — *"He does this for 45 minutes. Every day."* — and nothing else happens. Sound on is a bonus; the caption carries it muted.

### V2 — "Backwards" · 30 sec
The master film's structure, compressed and made explicit. Six images running in reverse with a timecode counting *down* in the corner: cup ← drying bed ← ferment ← cherry ← root ← cow. End card: *"That's the wrong way round. That's the point."*

### V3 — "Six lots" · 20 sec
The 30-second cut, restaged vertically — each lot a full-bleed frame with the name and one tasting note. Made for saving, not watching.

**Specs for all three:** open captions burned in, first frame legible as a still, no text in the bottom 15% (UI overlap), and the brand mark visible by 0:02.

## WEBSITE AMBIENT LOOPS

Silent, muted, `preload="none"`, playing on intersection. 8–12 seconds each, cut so the loop point is invisible.

| Loop | Footage | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Steam** | The cup, top-down, steam only, no hands | Microsite movement 00 |
| **Vortex** | The stir, mid-cycle, 10 sec | Movement 04 — the loop |
| **Canopy** | Light moving through four stories | Movement 05 — the light |
| **Foam** | Ferment surface, macro | Movement 08 — the six |
| **Eye** | The cow, blinking, no cut | Movement 03 — the herd |

Each must read as a still on first paint — the poster frame has to be a good photograph on its own, because on a slow connection that is all anyone will ever see.

---

## WHAT NONE OF THESE DO

- No cutdown ends on a web address read aloud.
- No cutdown uses music except the six-second bumpers, which use none either.
- No cutdown makes a claim the master film doesn't. Everything here is already covered in [claims-and-proof.md](../01_STRATEGY/claims-and-proof.md).
- No cutdown is generated by an editor working alone from the master timeline. Each one is a brief.

---

## PRODUCTION ORDER

If the budget only stretches to some of this, build in this order:

1. **The 15-second "Four legs."** Cheapest, most shareable, needs one shot.
2. **The four 6-second bumpers.** Same footage, four hours of edit.
3. **The website loops.** Free — they come out of the master's rushes.
4. **The 30-second "Six ways."** Needs the six lot images shot properly.
5. **The 60-second "The stir."** Needs the master's centrepiece shot to have worked.
6. **The three verticals.** Last, and only if the master film has landed.
