## ACT ONE — WHERE AM I?

---

**0:00 — 0:06**

**VISUAL**
Black, two seconds. Then a cup of black coffee, top-down, filling most of frame. Shallow. Steam moving across the surface. Close enough that it reads as an object before it reads as a drink. No hands, no table edge, no context. `[SHOOT]`

**AUDIO**
Room tone. A building somewhere, faintly. No music.

**VOICEOVER**
—

---

**0:06 — 0:14**

**VISUAL**
A hand enters from the right, takes the cup, lifts it out of frame. Camera does not follow. What is left is a wet ring on dark wood. Hold on the ring for three full seconds. `[SHOOT]`

**AUDIO**
Ceramic on wood. A chair. Someone breathing out.

**VOICEOVER**
—

---

**0:14 — 0:22**

**VISUAL**
Match cut on the circle: the ring becomes a raised drying bed, seen from directly overhead — same geometry, same warm tone, parchment instead of liquid. A wooden rake enters and pulls a furrow through it. `[SHOOT — but `/journals/coffee/` has close bean stills that can bridge]`

**AUDIO**
The estate arrives all at once: birds, insects, wind in the canopy, beans moving. Loud, then settling.

**VOICEOVER**
—

---

**0:22 — 0:32**

**VISUAL**
Stay overhead. Wider now — the bed runs out of frame in both directions. A second rake. Two people working, seen only as hands and forearms.

**AUDIO**
As above. Rhythmic raking.

**VOICEOVER**
> *This is the last thing that happened.*

---

**0:32 — 0:44**

**VISUAL**
Cut to dark. Interior, the wet mill. Sealed steel tank. Slow push on the surface of a ferment — fine foam lifting and collapsing, a slick moving. Almost abstract. `[SHOOT — the film's second-most-important shot]`

**AUDIO**
Close, wet, granular. Gas escaping. A drip. Room gone quiet.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Before it, a week in the dark.*

---

**0:44 — 0:52**

**VISUAL**
A refractometer lifted to a window. A thumb wipes it. A pH probe going into the tank, the number settling. Three quick cuts, all close, all hands. `[SHOOT]`

**AUDIO**
Glass, liquid, a small electronic tone from the meter.

**VOICEOVER**
—

---

**0:52 — 1:06**

**VISUAL**
Exterior, morning. Hands picking. Fingers moving over a branch, taking two cherries and leaving three. Then a basket, filling. Then a single unripe cherry being flicked away.
`[EXISTS — partial: /journals/coffee/aura-dry-osmosis.jpg for reference; motion must be shot]`

**AUDIO**
Cloth, leaves, the small dull sound of cherry into basket. Voices in Kannada, distant, not subtitled.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Before that, a morning*
> *when somebody decided this cherry was ready*
> *and that one wasn't.*

## ACT TWO — WHAT AM I WATCHING?

---

**1:06 — 1:14**

**VISUAL**
Tilt up the trunk of a coffee plant into the canopy. Light breaking through four stories of leaf. Slow. The first time the film looks up. `[EXISTS — /shade/videos/aura-tree-canopy-lookup.mp4]`

Then one cut: a lux meter held flat under the canopy, its display legible. `[SHOOT]`

**AUDIO**
Wind through leaves. Everything else drops away.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Before that, light.*
> *We measure it before we cut.*

---

**1:14 — 1:22**

**VISUAL**
Down. A hand pushes mulch aside at the base of a coffee plant and sets a ball of dark compost against the root, then covers it. One continuous shot, no cuts. `[EXISTS — /circular/images/aura-biodynamic.jpg as reference; motion must be shot]`

**AUDIO**
Soil, leaf litter, breath. Then nothing.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Everything you just watched*
> *was made by an animal.*

*Then eight seconds of silence. The longest hold in the film.*

---

**1:22 — 1:30**

**VISUAL**
Still silent. Three shots, slow, held:
· fungi fruiting through mulch `[EXISTS — /ecology/images/aura-white-mushrooms-mulch.jpg]`
· an earthworm in a hand `[SHOOT]`
· microbial colonies under a lens `[EXISTS — /circular/videos/aura-microbe.mp4]`

**AUDIO**
Room tone only. No estate, no music. The audio floor drops so far that the audience leans in.

**VOICEOVER**
—

---

**1:30 — 1:52**

**VISUAL**
**The centrepiece.** Interior, the preparation shed. A man stirring a barrel of Jeevamrit with a wooden pole — building the vortex, breaking it, reversing. **One unbroken shot, twenty-two seconds, locked off.** No cutaways. The camera does not move. `[SHOOT — the single most important shot in the film]`

The reference frame exists: `/circular/images/aura-jeevamrut.jpg`. It needs to be motion, and it needs to be long enough to become hypnotic.

**AUDIO**
Water turning. Wood against steel. Effort in the breathing. This is the only shot in the film where the sound alone would hold you.

**VOICEOVER**
> *(at 1:34, over the shot)*
> *She eats the same ground the coffee grows in.*
> *What she takes from it, she gives back.*

---

**1:52 — 2:04**

**VISUAL**
The loop closes in six fast cuts, each roughly two seconds, each one a step further back:
grass under hoof → dung at dawn → the numbered pit → the barrel → soil at a root → a cherry on the branch.
`[EXISTS — /herd/images/aura-grassland2.jpg · /circular/images/aura-dung.jpg · aura-cpp.jpg · aura-jeevamrut.jpg; all need motion equivalents]`

**AUDIO**
Rhythm builds from the estate's own sounds — the raking, the stirring, hooves — layered into something almost percussive, then cut dead.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Nothing comes in.*
> *Nothing leaves.*

## ACT THREE — WHY IS THIS DIFFERENT?

---

**2:04 — 2:12**

**VISUAL**
A Malnad Gidda, close, three-quarter. She looks at the lens and keeps looking. Held for a full six seconds with no cut — long enough to be uncomfortable, and then long enough to be funny. `[EXISTS — /journals/living-systems/aura-cow-eye.mp4 · /herd/videos/aura-relationship2.mp4]`

**AUDIO**
Breathing. A bell, once, far off. Nothing else.

**VOICEOVER**
> *We don't buy fertility.*
> *We make it.*

---

**2:12 — 2:20**

**VISUAL**
Return. The cup, top-down, exactly the frame the film opened on — same lens, same height, same wood. The wet ring is still there beside it. A hand comes in and lifts it toward camera. `[SHOOT — shoot with the opening]`

**AUDIO**
The room from 0:06 returns. Ceramic. Breath.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Coffee that leaves the land*
> *better than it found it.*

---

**2:20 — 2:24**

**VISUAL**
Cut to black one frame before the cup reaches the mouth.

**AUDIO**
Cut with it. Total silence.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Is that a thing?*

---

**2:24 — 2:30**

**VISUAL**
Black. Then, one line at a time:

> **Aura presents**
> **Regenerative Coffee.**
> **One remarkable circle.**

The hand-drawn ring closes beneath the last line.

Then, small and centred, four seconds:

> theaura.life

**AUDIO**
One low sustained note, entering under *"Yes"* and decaying through the card. Nothing else.

**VOICEOVER**
> *Yes. It is.*

# THE FIVE SHOTS THE FILM CANNOT BE MADE WITHOUT

Everything else is replaceable. These are not.

1. **The stir, unbroken, 22 seconds.** The centre of the film.
2. **The cup, top-down, opening and closing.** The circle.
3. **The compost ball going in at the root.** The turn at 1:14.
4. **The ferment surface, foam lifting.** The only truly unfamiliar image in the film.
5. **The cow, held six seconds, looking back.** The payoff.

All five can be shot in two days at Mudigere during harvest. None of them require a crew larger than three.
