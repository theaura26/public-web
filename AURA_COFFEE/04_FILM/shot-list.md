## TECHNICAL

**Camera.** One body. 24 fps, log, 4K minimum. Prime lenses — 35, 50, 85, and a 100 macro. No zooms; the film's grammar is fixed focal lengths and locked frames.

**Movement.** Tripod for everything except the picking shots. Two slow pushes in the entire film (05 and 13). **No gimbal, no slider, no drone.** If a shot needs movement to be interesting, it is the wrong shot.

**Light.** Available only. The estate under four stories of canopy is dim, green-grey and low contrast — shoot for that, not against it. One bounce for the interiors. The only bright frames in the film are the drying beds and the cup.

**Grade.** Hold the shadows. Do not lift, do not warm, do not saturate the greens. The cup is the only genuinely warm image.

**Sound.** Record clean audio on every setup, including the shots that will run muted. The film's sound design is built almost entirely from location recording — see [sound-direction.md](sound-direction.md). Bring a shotgun, a pair of lavs for the shed, and a contact mic for the barrel.

**Shoot window.** December or January. September gives the ground and the canopy but no cherry, no ferment, and no cup — which removes eleven of the twenty-seven shots.
