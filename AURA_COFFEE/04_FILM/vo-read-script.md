# VO — Read Script
## Aura Regenerative Coffee · 2:30

**This is the page you hand to the reader.** Nothing on it explains the film. Print it at A4, one side, large.

`/` = short breath · `//` = full stop, let it sit · **bold** = the only stressed word in the line

---

```
                                                    01
        This is the last thing / that happened.


                                                    02
        Before it, / a week in the dark.


                                                    03
        Before that, a morning /
        when somebody decided this cherry was ready /
        and that one wasn't.


                                                    04
        Before that, / light.  //
        We measure it before we cut.


                                                    05
        Everything you just watched /
        was made by an animal.  //////


                                                    06
        She eats the same ground the coffee grows in. /
        What she takes from it, / she gives back.


                                                    07
        Nothing comes in.  //
        Nothing leaves.


                                                    08
        We don't buy fertility.  //
        We make it.


                                                    09
        Coffee that leaves the land /
        better than it found it.


                                                    10
        Is that a thing?


                                                    11
        Yes.  //  It is.
```

---

## THE FOUR PASSES

Read the whole thing through four times. Don't stop to fix anything.

**Pass 1 — as written.** Straight through, no interpretation.

**Pass 2 — faster.** About 20% quicker than feels right. This kills any tendency to perform.

**Pass 3 — to a sceptic.** Read it as if explaining to a friend who has just said *"come on, that's marketing."* Slightly defensive, slightly amused.

**Pass 4 — half volume.** Almost under your breath, close to the mic. This is usually the one that gets used.

---

## THE ONLY THREE NOTES THAT MATTER

**Line 01 must not sound clever.** It is the line that tells the audience the film is running backwards, and it has to be thrown away completely. If it lands, the trick is spoiled in the first four seconds.

**Line 05 is a fact, not a reveal.** No lift on *animal*. Say it the way you'd say the time. Then stop — there are eight seconds of silence after it, and they only work if you don't fill them.

**Line 08 is the proudest thing in the film, so say it like the least proud.** Plain, slightly tired, the voice of someone who did the job this morning and is not looking for credit.

---

## ALTERNATES

Read the primary first. These exist so there's a choice in the edit, not because the primary is weak.

| # | Primary | Alternate |
|---|---|---|
| 02 | Before it, a week in the dark. | Before it, six days in a sealed tank. |
| 03 | …when somebody decided this cherry was ready and that one wasn't. | …when somebody left three on the branch and took two. |
| 05 | Everything you just watched was made by an animal. | All of that started in a cow. |
| 06 | She eats the same ground the coffee grows in. | She eats the same hundred and fifty acres the coffee grows in. |
| 09 | Coffee that leaves the land better than it found it. | Coffee that leaves the ground better than it found it. |
| 11 | Yes. It is. | It is, actually. |

**Line 05's alternate is deliberately blunter.** Worth recording both — the short version may carry the turn better against picture, and you cannot tell until it is cut.

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO IN THE ROOM

- No booth. A room with soft furniture, sitting down, close to the mic.
- No headphones playback between takes. It makes people self-correct toward "announcer".
- Don't read along with picture. The edit is cut to the voice, not the other way round.
- Don't fix a line more than twice. If it isn't working after two goes, move on and come back at the end.

---

## ALSO RECORD IN THE SAME SESSION

These are the cutdown and social lines. They are short, they use the same voice, and going back for them later never sounds the same. Record them cold, at the end, after the fourth pass.

### 60-second cut — "The stir"

```
        This is how we make fertility.  //
        We don't buy it.
```

*Same register as line 08 of the master. Plain, unbothered.*

### 30-second cut — "Six ways"

```
        The same cherry, / picked the same morning.  //
        Six different coffees.
```

*Slightly quicker than the master. This one is allowed to sound interested.*

### 15-second cut — "Four legs"

```
        Our best farmers / have four legs.
```

*The only line in the whole system with a joke in it. Do not sell it — say it completely straight and let the picture be funny.*

**Record this one five times.** It carries the most-shared asset in the campaign and the read has to be exactly right.

### Six-second bumpers — no voice

All four run silent. Nothing to record.

### Vertical socials — no voice

Captions carry them. Nothing to record.
