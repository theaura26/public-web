# Content Gaps
## What is missing, what it blocks, and what it costs to close

Everything in this project was written from Aura's own published site (this repository, `theaura.life`). That is a rich source — six documented lots, a full preparation programme, a 2026 lux survey, per-animal records — but it is a *statement* of the facts, not the underlying evidence. This document lists what has to arrive before the work can publish.

Ordered by how much each gap unblocks.

---

## TIER 1 — BLOCKS PUBLICATION

### 1. The Experience — September, December, January
**Blocks:** the microsite's only call to action, keynote slide 32, the entire `/experience` page.

Nothing in any source material describes what these three months are. The copy currently in [website-copy.md](../02_MICROSITE/website-copy.md) is written on the assumption that they follow the Karnataka Arabica calendar — late monsoon, first pick, cupping — and is marked `[NEEDS SOURCE]` throughout.

**Needed:**
- What is each of the three? Harvest experience, residency, cupping week, gathering?
- Duration, and exact dates
- Who is it for — buyers, roasters, guests, all three?
- Capacity per season
- Price, or "by invitation"
- Where do people stay?
- Is the cupping room ready, or is that `[FUTURE]`?

**Cost to close:** one conversation.

---

### 2. One complete, published lot file
**Blocks:** the *Transparent* pillar — a third of the entire proposition.

This is the single highest-value missing asset in the project. A real, public, clickable page for one lot — block, pick date, method, ferment log, drying, cupping notes — converts the transparency pillar from an assertion into evidence, and **removes any need to mention a blockchain at all.**

**Cost to close:** the data already exists. This is a page build, not a research task.

---

### 3. Harvest year for the six lots
**Blocks:** `/lots`, keynote slides 25–26, the microsite's six-lot movement.

Every lot figure on the live site — 11,984 kg, Brix 19.55%, 22 days drying — is undated. A green buyer's first question will be *which harvest*, and there is currently no answer.

**Cost to close:** one field in a spreadsheet.

---

### 4. Three factual corrections on the live site
**Blocks:** nothing downstream, but each is a credibility risk that grows with traffic.

| Claim | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| *"World's Oldest Arabica Region, Rediscovered"* | Not true — Arabica originates in Ethiopia; Yemen precedes India by centuries | *"Where Indian coffee began."* |
| *"UNESCO biodiversity zone"* as an estate attribute | The Western Ghats WHS is a serial property of 39 components; being in the region ≠ being inside one | *"In the Western Ghats, a UNESCO World Heritage region."* |
| Two different coordinate pairs | `13.1365°N, 75.6403°E` on `/land` vs `13.168594, 75.433983` on `/mudigere-estate` — ~23 km apart | Confirm one, correct the other |

Full detail in [claims-and-proof.md](../01_STRATEGY/claims-and-proof.md).

---

### 5. Blockchain — verify or restate
**Blocks:** honest use of the *Transparent* pillar.

The site currently states that BD cycles are *"sealed on the blockchain"* and milestones *"written to chain — permanent, public, verifiable."* There is no chain named, no contract address, and no link a buyer can click.

**Either** publish a public verification link, **or** restate both claims as `[FUTURE]`. There is no third option, and item 2 above makes the claim unnecessary anyway.

## TIER 2 — WEAKENS THE PROOF

### 6. One dated lab report
A single preparation batch, tested before and after — pH, EC, colony counts, *Trichoderma*, *Pseudomonas*. This is the keystone evidence behind *"no untested material touches the soil"*, which is the strongest sentence Aura owns.

### 7. One soil before/after pair
One block, tested at application and again at ninety days. Proves the loop closes.

### 8. A third-party cupping score
80+ SCA is currently a **target** on the live site. Until a real score exists it must be worded as a target everywhere — which means the *Flavourful* pillar is currently making its weakest available argument.

### 9. Carbon: 3–5× or 4–5×?
Both figures appear across the site for the same claim, with no published methodology. Pick one, publish the method, and always use the word *estimated*.

### 10. Ecological Health Index — running or designed?
Fourteen monitoring programmes and a 0–100 per-block index are described in the present tense. Confirm how many are actually collecting data and how many blocks carry a scored index with a date. If it is partial, say so — a pilot honestly described is worth more than a system implied.

### 11. Written consent
From Nayana, Jagadeshwari, Raju, Dr Arun, and every worker whose face is identifiable in the photography or film. Non-negotiable before publication.

## TIER 3 — WOULD MAKE THE WORK BETTER

### 12. A before/after pair of one regenerated block
Same block, same angle, two or more years apart. If a dated pair exists in anyone's phone it is worth more than most of the shot list. This is the one asset that would let the work *show* regeneration rather than describe it.

### 13. Named people, on the record
The film and the site both improve enormously with one or two real voices. See [interview-questions.md](interview-questions.md).

### 14. The founder's card
The original handwritten card — *"Regenerative coffee. Is that a thing?"* — photographed, and the ring and three underlines vectorised. The entire visual system hangs off it.

### 15. What has gone wrong
Aura's honesty about the Solera question is the most persuasive material in the whole body of work. One more example — a failed batch, a block that did not improve, a ferment that ran too warm — would be worth more than any new success story.
