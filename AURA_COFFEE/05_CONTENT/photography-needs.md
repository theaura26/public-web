# Photography Needs
## One shoot list, ordered by what it unblocks

Roughly two thirds of the imagery this project needs already exists in `/public`. This document covers the third that doesn't.

The per-section breakdown of what exists lives in [asset-requirements.md](../02_MICROSITE/asset-requirements.md). This is the shoot brief.

---

## THE FOUR THAT CHANGE WHAT THE WORK IS

Without these, the microsite and the deck are well-written descriptions. With them, they are evidence.

### 1. The cupping table, overhead
Six cups, six spoons, six lot cards, shot from directly above. Available light. The whole proposition in one frame — and the payoff of nine movements of farming.

**Currently:** there is no strong tasting imagery in the library at all. This is the single biggest hole.
**Needs:** January, when lots are on the table.

### 2. The lux meter, number legible
A hand holding a digital lux meter flat under the canopy, the display readable. Ideally Nayana or Jagadeshwari, credited.

**Why it matters:** "measure first, prune later" is the line that separates Aura from every farm with a nice story, and it is currently unillustrated.

### 3. Signing from the field
A thumb on a tablet at first light, mud on the hand, canopy behind. This is a grey placeholder on the live `/provenance` page today.

**Why it matters:** it is the only image that makes the record feel human rather than administrative.

### 4. The ear tag, macro
Yellow tag, a number legible, the animal's ear and hide filling the frame.

**Why it matters:** one frame that carries the entire passport claim.

## THE ONE THAT MAY NOT BE POSSIBLE

**A before/after pair of one block.** Same block, same angle, two or more years apart.

This sits outside the ranking because it cannot be shot — it either exists already or it doesn't. If a dated pair is on anyone's phone, in any quality, it is worth more than items 5 through 11 combined. It is the only way to *show* regeneration rather than describe it.

**Ask everyone.** Field staff, Dr Arun, the founder, the interns. Someone has it.

## THE FOUNDER'S CARD

Not a shoot so much as a scan, but it is the origin of the entire visual system.

- The physical card photographed flat, raking light, paper texture and ink visible
- The scribbled ring vectorised, wobble intact
- The three underlines (*Biodynamic*, *Transparent*, *Flavourful*) vectorised **separately** — they must not become three identical strokes

---

## HOW IT SHOULD LOOK

**Light.** Available only. Dawn and late afternoon. The canopy makes midday flat and green.

**Register.** The estate at 3,600 feet under four stories of canopy is a dim place — green-grey, humid, low contrast. Shoot for that. Expose for highlight and let the shadows go. The only genuinely bright frames in the whole system are the drying beds and the cup.

**Distance.** Close. Hands, hide, bark, foam, soil, steam. The library is already strong on landscape and weak on touch.

**No styling.** Nothing arranged for camera. If a frame looks composed, it stops being evidence.

---

## DELIVERY SPEC

Every hero shot in **landscape and 4:5 portrait** — the film cutdowns, the deck and the site all need both.

Per asset:
- Original RAW
- Web-optimised AVIF/WebP, ≤1600 px long edge
- 4:5 crop · 16:9 crop
- Alt text written by whoever was standing there

**Naming:** follow the existing convention — `aura-{subject}-{detail}.jpg`, lower case, hyphenated. Store under `/public/coffee/` so it doesn't collide with the journals.

**Consent:** written release from every identifiable person, and from anyone named. Non-negotiable before publication.

---

## WHEN TO SHOOT

**December or January.** The harvest window gives the cherry, the ferment, the drying beds and the cup — which is eight of the eleven shots above.

September gives the ground, the canopy, the preparations and the herd, and none of the coffee. If there is budget for only one trip, it is not September.
