# Video Needs
## What has to be filmed, and what already exists

The full shot list with timings and technical spec is [shot-list.md](../04_FILM/shot-list.md). This is the summary for whoever is planning and budgeting the shoot.

---

## THE HEADLINE

**Seventeen of the film's twenty-seven shots must be captured.** Seven exist. Three are edit-only (black, end card, lock-up).

But the number that matters is smaller: **five shots, two days, a crew of three.** Everything else is a bonus, and most of the bonus already exists.

## TECHNICAL

**Camera.** One body. 24 fps, log, 4K minimum. Primes only — 35, 50, 85, 100 macro. No zooms.

**Movement.** Tripod for everything except picking. Two slow pushes in the entire film. **No gimbal, no slider, no drone.** There are zero aerial shots in this film by design; if a shot needs movement to hold attention, it is the wrong shot.

**Light.** Available only, one bounce for interiors. Shoot for the dim green-grey of a four-story canopy rather than against it.

**Grade.** Hold the shadows. Do not lift, warm, or saturate the greens.

**Audio — the part most likely to be under-resourced.** Record clean sound on every setup, including shots that will run muted. The film has nine seconds of music and roughly 48 seconds of silence; everything else is location recording.

Bring: a shotgun, two lavs, a contact mic for the barrel, a stereo pair for ambience.

**Do this and nothing else will matter as much:** wild-track the stir for five full minutes, separate from the picture take, and record thirty minutes of clean estate ambience at dawn, mid-morning and dusk with the camera off and nobody moving. That library is worth more than an extra camera day.

## WEBSITE LOOPS — FREE, FROM THE SAME RUSHES

Five silent 8–12 second loops, cut so the loop point is invisible: **steam · vortex · canopy · foam · eye.**

Each needs a poster frame that works as a still photograph on its own, because on a slow connection that is all most people will see.

---

## SCHEDULE

**December or January**, inside the harvest, when the wet mill is running and the beds are full.

September gives the ground, the canopy, the preparations and the herd — and removes eleven of the twenty-seven shots, including the cup, the ferment and all six lots. If there is one trip, it is not September.

**Two days is enough** for the five priority shots plus most of the supporting list. A third day buys the drying beds at three different times and the ambience library.

**Crew of three:** camera, sound, and one person who knows the estate and can get you to the shed before the light changes. Anything larger and the film starts to look staged — which is the one thing it cannot survive.

## CONSENT

Written release from every person whose face or hands appear, including the stirrer, the pickers and the caretaker, and from anyone named on screen or in the credits. Get it at the start of the day, not the end.
