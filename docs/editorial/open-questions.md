# Open questions

What the site cannot currently prove, what it needs in order to say more,
and what somebody has to decide. Each entry names the pages affected and
the single artefact that would close it.

The rule this register exists to enforce: **a page short of evidence stays
short.** Nothing here is written around, padded out, or upgraded from
intention to achievement while it waits.

## 1. Held for the next edition

### 1.1 The lab record for preparations

**Pages:** `/circular`, `/regenerative-life/biodynamic`, `/regenerative-life/vedic-farming`, `/regenerative-coffee/biodynamic`

The site says every batch of CPP and Jeevamrit is tested before and after —
pH, EC, colony counts, *Trichoderma*, *Pseudomonas* — and that a failing
batch is corrected or held. This is the strongest claim in the whole
system and the one that makes the rest believable.

**Needed:** one exported batch sheet, any batch, with the before/after pair
and the operator's signature. Redact nothing but the name if consent is
outstanding.

**Until then:** stated as estate practice with the record described, never
as published data.

### 1.2 The 90-day soil re-read

**Pages:** `/circular`, `/regenerative-life/soil`, `/vedic`

A block is retested ninety days after application, against the batch that
went on it. The before/after pair has never been shown.

**Needed:** one block, one pair of results, one date.

### 1.3 The tree count

**Pages:** `/land`

"35,000 individual trees" is published as an estimate. Tree tagging is in
progress.

**Needed:** the completed count, or a stated coverage figure ("tagged to
date: N of an estimated 35,000").

### 1.4 The carbon multiple

**Pages:** `/land`, `/coffee`, `/provenance`, `/living-systems`

"Estimated 4–5× the carbon of monoculture coffee" appears site-wide, always
worded as an estimate. The method behind it is not published.

**Needed:** the basis — which pools, which comparison, whose coefficients.
One paragraph would move this from estimate to reading.

### 1.5 The Ecological Health Index

**Pages:** `/ecology`

Fourteen monitoring programmes are described as rolling into an index
scored 0–100 per block. Not every block carries a scored index and no
estate-wide figure is published.

**Needed:** confirmation of which programmes are live this season, and one
scored block as a worked example. The page currently describes a design;
it should say which parts are running.

## How to use this file

When a page cannot say something, the gap goes here and a short version of
it goes into the page's own *what we can't prove yet* block. A reader who
can see where the record stops has a reason to trust the part that does not.

When a gap closes, move the row to §4 with what closed it, and update
`claims-ledger.md` in the same commit.
